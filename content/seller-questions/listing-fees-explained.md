Sellers consistently tell us the same thing: they don't mind fees, they mind *not knowing* the fees. So here is every number, in one place, in one worked example.

## The plans

- **Per-item plan:** flat **$0.30** when an item sells. Nothing when it doesn't. No monthly fee at all.
- **Bench plan:** **$9/month** + 2% per sale, for anyone listing dozens of items monthly. It only makes sense above roughly 35 sales a month; the checkout counter on your seller page tells you where you stand.
- Payment processing (the card networks' cut) is a separate ~2.9% + $0.30 that goes to the processor, not to us. It shows on the same receipt so you can see the whole picture.

## The example in plain numbers

```text
Item sells for              $12.50
Maple Lane fee (per-item)   $0.30
Processor fee (2.9% + $0.30) $0.66
---------------------------
You receive                 $11.54
```

That's what the money looks like. The same sale under the Bench plan comes out better only once your volume justifies the monthly — the seller dashboard does that math for you, honestly, even when the honest answer is "stay on per-item".

## Where to see your fees

1. Open **Seller dashboard** on your account page.
2. Each completed sale lists its fee lines right next to the sale amount.
3. The monthly summary email shows the same numbers per-week, plus a plain-English total.

## What the fee actually buys

Payment processing and fraud checks, the discounted shipping rates, the buyer-protection fund (this is the part that answers [when a buyer's parcel goes missing](../orders-shipping/track-my-package.html)), and plain-English tax summaries in February.

> We'd rather tell you the exact numbers and lose small than have you discover fees at payout time. That's also why this article reads like a recipe card instead of a contract.

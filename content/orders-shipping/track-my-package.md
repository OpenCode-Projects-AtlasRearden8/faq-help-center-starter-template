Every shipped order gets a shipping confirmation email with a tracking link in it. That link is the real deal — it goes to the carrier's own tracking, not a pretty copy in our shop.

## Step by step

1. Open the email with the subject **"Your Maple Lane order has shipped"**.
2. Click the big tracking button or the link under "Track your parcel".
3. The carrier page shows the current status and the expected delivery date.

## Reading tracking statuses without anxiety

- **Pre-shipment / label created** — we've made the label; the parcel hasn't been scanned yet. This can sit for a few hours and is normal.
- **In transit** — moving through the network. Between scans it may look frozen for a day or more. Frozen isn't lost.
- **Out for delivery** — on the truck. Genuine "hold your breath by the window" hours.
- **Delivered** — checked at the door (or the porch, or the mail room).

## If tracking hasn't moved in several days

One to two quiet days is normal. Three or more begins to be worth a look:

1. Wait one more full business day — "stuck" tracking often unsticks overnight.
2. Still nothing? Read [What do I do if my package is late?](package-arrived-late.html).
3. At five days of silence, email us. Tracking that never updates usually means the parcel was mis-scanned; we can open a carrier search from our side much faster than you can fight their phone tree.

escape: If the carrier's own tracking page shows an error or refuses to load, that's on the carrier's site — tell us in an email and we'll pull the record from our side.

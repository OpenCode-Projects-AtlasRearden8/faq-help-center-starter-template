We're sorry. Now let's fix it fast: this is the shortest queue in our inbox.

## What to do (about four minutes)

1. **Keep everything** — the item, the box, the padding. Carriers require the original packaging for damage claims; missing boxes mean missing refunds.
2. **Snap 2–3 photos**: one of the damage up close, one of the item as a whole, one of the shipping box with the label visible.
3. Email the photos to us (or reply to your order confirmation) with your order number and one line: *"arrived damaged"* or *"missing X"*.

## What we do

- **Damaged on arrival** we replace first — you shouldn't have to wait for a refund dance for something you clearly wanted. Want money back instead? Say so, done.
- **Missing pieces** we ship the missing part directly — no need to send the whole thing back.

You have **14 days** from the delivery date to report it; accidents do happen to us too, so a few days late is still worth a try.

> We never ask you to prove the damage twice or fill out three forms. One email, the photos, the order number — that's the whole process.

escape: If photo uploads strip or bounce on your email app, just describe it in words to start with — we'll sort out the pictures over the next reply.

The short version: we hand your order to the carrier within **two business days** of your order, and the shipping time from there depends on the option you picked at checkout.

## What "two business days" means

- It counts Monday through Friday, not weekends or holidays.
- It starts the day *after* you order — an order placed Friday evening starts its count Monday morning.
- Personalized items and gift wrapping add one business day (it's real hands-on work, not a machine setting).

## How to check your order

1. Open the **shipping confirmation email** we sent you (subject line starts with "Your Maple Lane order").
2. It includes a live tracking link. Tracking numbers become active once the carrier scans the parcel — sometimes a few hours after we ship.
3. No email? Check your spam folder for `@example.com`. Still nothing after a full day? That's on us — [email us](mailto:help@example.com) with your name and approximate order date and we'll find it.

## If the date matters

If you genuinely need this by a specific date — a birthday, a movie night, a repair appointment — we'd rather know now than have you cross your fingers. Reply to your order confirmation and we'll tell you honestly whether the date is realistic.

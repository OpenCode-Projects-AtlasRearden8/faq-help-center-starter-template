Deep breath. Between 1 and 3 days of quiet tracking is completely normal; carriers hit bumps and don't always narrate them. Here's the timeline we use ourselves.

## Day 0–2: let it ride

Tracking between scans often sits still. Do not cancel your evening plans over it.

## Day 3–4: worth a nudge

Email us with your order number. We can see the parcel's scans on our side and will open a carrier search. We also know the special sting of tracking that says "Delivered!" while your porch is empty.

## Day 5+: our problem, formal version

If the carrier search comes up empty (marking it lost), we don't make you fill out paperwork for a parcel you never got: we replace it or refund it, your choice. You will never re-buy something because the post office had a bad week.

> If tracking says **"Delivered"** but nothing has appeared: check with neighbors, mail rooms, and anyone in the house who "didn't order anything" (there is always one). Then give it until the next business day before we start a trace — packages do occasionally scurry home on their own.

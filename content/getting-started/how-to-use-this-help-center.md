Welcome! Everything here is written the way a friend would explain it: plain words, short steps, no jargon and no runaround.

## Search first (it's fast)

Type a few plain words into the search box at the top of any page. Results appear as you type — no button to press. A few habits that help:

- Short beats long. `refund` works better than `I want to know about my refund status`.
- Try the obvious noun. `tracking`, `card`, `return`, `fees`.
- Misspelled slightly? Try again — the search is literal, not magical.

## Or browse by category

Below the search box, questions are grouped by category, like a well-organized drawer:

- **Orders & Shipping** — where my stuff is and when it arrives
- **Returns & Refunds** — sending things back and getting money back
- **Payments & Accounts** — cards, receipts, duplicate charges
- **Seller Questions** — selling through our marketplace

## Every answer has a way out

Here's our rule: no dead ends. Every article — including this one — ends with a short **"Didn't find your answer?"** box that gives you a direct route to a human. If you read something and it didn't work, that's a bug in our article, not in you. Tell us, and we'll fix it.

## One more trick

Press the `/` key anywhere on a page to jump straight into the search box. Press `Esc` to clear it.

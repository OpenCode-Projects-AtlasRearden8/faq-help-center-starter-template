If you've searched and browsed and still have a question mark over your head, email us. Seriously — that's what the box on every page is for.

## Where to go

Use the **"Didn't find your answer?"** box at the bottom of whichever article came closest, or go straight to [Contact us](../contact.html). Either way you reach the same inbox: `help@example.com`.

## What to write (the two-minute recipe)

1. **What you were trying to do**, in one sentence.
2. **What you tried.** "I followed the returns article and the form wouldn't load for me" is perfect.
3. **Any order number or reference** involved — it's in your confirmation email.
4. **A screenshot** if your eye saw the problem. Pictures save everyone time.

You do not need to write politely-perfect prose. Bullet points in your normal voice are exactly right. And time-sensitive matters (a damaged parcel, a payment you want checked) go to the front of the queue — the same rules that decide that are the [short version of our return policy](../returns-refunds/return-policy.html#the-short-version).

## What happens next

- We reply from the same email address within one business day (usually much faster).
- If something is broken, we say so plainly and tell you what we're doing about it — no "we value your patience" runaround.
- If three people ask the same thing, the fourth person won't have to: we write a new article from the question.

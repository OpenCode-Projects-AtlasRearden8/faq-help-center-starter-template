Starting a return is deliberately boring. It's one email and one envelope — no phone calls, no chat roulette, no forms that ask who bagged your groceries.

## What you need before you start

- The paper slip inside the box, or the order confirmation email (either works — the order number is the key)
- The item, and ideally its original box and padding
- A moment with a tape dispenser

## The steps

1. **Email us** at [help@example.com](mailto:help@example.com) with the subject "Return", the order number, and one line about why. Be blunt — "my kitchen has no room for it" is a perfectly good reason and helps us stock better.
2. We reply, usually within a day, with a pre-filled shipping label PDF and the address.
3. **Print the label, tape the box, drop it** at any post office or pickup point. No printer? The drop point staff can print it from the code in our email.
4. That's it. When the parcel's scan shows it arrived back at our place, we send your refund — here's the timing of [when the refund lands](refund-timing.html).

## What if the item is damaged or wrong?

Don't start a return for that — it's faster and cheaper for everyone. See [Item arrived damaged or incomplete](../orders-shipping/item-arrived-damaged.html) instead. We send a replacement first and pay all shipping.

escape: If you can't find the order number anywhere, don't start over — email us your name and the rough date you ordered, and we'll dig it up ourselves.

Shortest article in this help center, because the waiting part isn't really ours.

## The timeline

- **Us:** your refund goes out within **one business day** of the returned parcel's arrival scan, back to the original payment card.
- **Your bank:** that's where patience comes in. Card refunds typically post in 3–5 business days; some banks sit on refunds for a week to ten and post at statement time.

So the honest full answer is: **3–10 business days** after your parcel gets scanned in, most of that being banks being banks.

## How to check where it is

1. Look for our email with the subject **"Refund issued"** — that email is proof we sent it, and includes the amount and last four digits of the card.
2. Check *pending transactions* in your banking app first; refund postings often hide in there.
3. At 10 business days with no sign of it, email us — we can (and do) chase the processor, and we'll send the reference number your bank can trace with.

## Refunds for things we messed up

Ordered wrong, arrived damaged, refunded before you shipped anything back? Those all leave on the spot and skip the above waiting — the bank part still applies, because we can't speed up Visa's Tuesdays.

escape: If this article didn't cover your situation (split payments, gift cards, a card you've since replaced — all real cases we've handled), just email us; these have special answers, not special hoops.

We sell small things for everyday homes. Buying online is an act of trust; returning something that didn't work out is part of the deal. Keep it simple, that's the whole policy.

## The short version

- **30 days** from delivery, no hard feelings.
- **Opened and used is fine.** We only ask that it's not wrecked — burned, cracked, or sanded down by the dog.
- **Refunds go back to your card** — see [when refunds arrive](refund-timing.html).
- Return shipping is free for damaged or wrong items, and cheap (we split it) for "just not for me".

> Household note: if someone gave it to you as a gift, you can still return it — you'll just get store credit instead of the money, unless you also happen to be the one who paid, in which case nice save.

## A few honest exceptions

- Food-safe items that have been used we can't resell, but email us anyway — we'd rather help than apply a rule blindly.
- Bulk orders (10+ of the same item) get a custom plan instead of this policy.

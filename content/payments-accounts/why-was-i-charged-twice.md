Seeing two identical lines on a card statement makes your heart drop. Nine times out of ten it isn't money, it's banking theater. Let's sort it out calmly.

## The common (harmless) cause: the double appearance

- Card *pre-authorizations* ("pending") appear immediately when you pay, then are replaced by the real charge when it settles — sometimes showing alongside the settled charge for a few days. Two lines on the screen, but only one is money.
- Refresh or wait a day: the pending line almost always disappears by itself. Your bank app's "pending" section is where this play runs.

## One quick check

Compare the two lines' *dates*. If one says (pending) or shows today's date against the order date, that's the pre-auth twin. Case closed, no email needed — though we're glad you checked.

## When it's a real double charge

Two lines both posted (not pending) from us, days apart? That's a genuine mistake on our side, and here's what we do:

1. Email us the two lines' dates and amounts — a screenshot of the statement is ideal if you have one.
2. We check our processor's ledgers, confirm which charge is the duplicate, and refund it the same or next business day. Refunds for our mistakes are immediate; see [when refunds land](../returns-refunds/refund-timing.html) for the bank-side wait.
3. We never argue. We already know both lines exist; you don't need to prove what your bank statement plainly says.

## What we need in the email

Just the dates. Two dates is genuinely all we need.

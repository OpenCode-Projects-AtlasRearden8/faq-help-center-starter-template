Two places hold your details, one tiny trap worth knowing about.

## If you have an account (or saved details)

1. On the shop, open **Account** (top-right corner), then **Saved details**.
2. To swap a card, click the old card, then **Replace card** — we'll take you through your bank's 3D Secure step and back.
3. To change an address, click **Addresses**, then edit. The starred one is the default shipping address.
4. Hit **Save** before wandering off — the page politely reminds you, but scrolling fast is a real thing we all do.

## If you check out as a guest

There's nothing saved to update — your details travel with each order. Fix it **at the next checkout**, or tell us: if an order is still open, emailing us your new address within the first two business days works, and we'll catch it before the label prints.

## The one trap: cards that expire mid-year

If your card expired but you once checked "save for future orders", saved card details can silently fail at renewal time rather than announcing themselves. If a payment unexpectedly fails, this is the likeliest culprit — replace the card using the steps above.

> Moving house? Update your address *before* ordering, and if anything is already en route, read [What do I do if my package is late?](../orders-shipping/package-arrived-late.html).

escape: If your card cleared but the order page still says "unpaid", don't click pay again — check [why was I charged twice?](why-was-i-charged-twice.html) and email us instead. Payments that "took" but don't show are rare, and fixable in minutes.
